About us

Every Boxscore Media Group was founded by Doug Branson, a video and podcast producer with more than a decade running high-volume, multi-format sports content. Before launching Every Boxscore, Doug was Director of Production at a national network of more than 180 team-focused sports podcasts, and earlier produced shows for Yahoo Sports.

We combine that production experience with our own data tools, so every story we publish starts from what actually happened on the floor or field.
